{% comment %} ============================== FAQ ============================== {% endcomment %}

Questions we hear a lot

Answered straight.

Who is Ledgerwise built for?

Wholesale distributors, importers, and multi-warehouse teams who need sales, purchasing, warehouse, and finance to share one record instead of four disconnected tools.

Can it handle multiple companies or warehouses?

Yes. Companies, warehouses, and currencies are first-class, and each person's access is scoped to the ones they actually work with.

How does role-based access work?

Each person is assigned a role — owner, accountant, sales, purchasing, or warehouse — that decides which companies, documents, and posting actions they can use.

Is this a live product I can sign in to?

Yes — sign in to explore the connected workbench if you already have an account, or request access if you don't yet.

How is my data protected?

Every sign-in and every change to an account is recorded, and sensitive financial actions stay restricted to the roles responsible for them.
