One means "this cannot be closed"; the other means "somebody has to decide". Rendering both as red problems teaches people to click through both. - The reason box is not decoration. It is the only lasting record of who signed the period off and on what basis, so it is required and it is kept. - Reopening is separated from closing and reads as the heavier action, because it is: it changes figures other people may already have quoted. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
